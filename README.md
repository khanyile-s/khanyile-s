## About Me

I'm a molecular and cellular biologist with an MSc from Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU).

My research background includes cancer biology, neuroscience, astrocyte reprogramming, tumour microenvironments, patient-derived organoids and RNA sequencing.

I'm interested in how computational approaches can help answer biological questions, analyse complex datasets and make biomedical research more reproducible.

## Research Interests

- Cancer biology and tumour microenvironments
- Neuroscience and astrocyte biology
- Transcriptomics and RNA-seq
- Computational biology and biomedical data science
- Reproducible research and statistical analysis
